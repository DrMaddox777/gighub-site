FAQ

Is GigHub really free?

Yes! GigHub is completely free with all features available to every user. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases.

How do I export my data?

Go to Settings > Export Data, select the tax year, and tap "Export to CSV". You can save the file, email it, or open it in Excel or Numbers.

Is my data stored in the cloud?

No. All your data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect or transmit any personal data.

How does mileage calculation work?

Set your home address in Settings, then when you add a gig with a location, GigHub automatically calculates the round-trip mileage using Apple Maps. You can also enter mileage manually.

Can I sync gigs to my iPhone Calendar?

Yes! When adding or editing a gig, toggle "Add to iPhone Calendar" to create a matching calendar event with all your gig details.

System Requirements

GigHub requires iOS 17.0 or later.
