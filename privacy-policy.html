GigHub - Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Overview

GigHub is a gig management app for musicians and freelancers. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data GigHub accesses and how it is used.

Data Storage

All your data (gigs, artists, expenses, payments, and settings) is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. We do not operate servers and do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data remotely.

Onboarding Information

When you first open GigHub, we ask for basic profile information (name, email, city/state, instrument, and experience level). This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to any server.

Device Permissions

GigHub may request the following permissions:

  • Contacts: Used only to link artists to existing contacts on your phone. Only the contact identifier is stored, not the contact data itself.
  • Calendar: Used to optionally add gig events to your iPhone Calendar app. Only accessed when you choose to sync a gig.
  • Location (When In Use): Used only for geocoding addresses to calculate mileage. Your location is never tracked or stored remotely.

Third-Party Services

  • Apple MapKit: Used for address autocomplete suggestions. Search queries are processed by Apple per their privacy policy.

Data Export

The CSV export feature generates a file stored locally on your device. You control how and where you share this file.

Children's Privacy

GigHub is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
